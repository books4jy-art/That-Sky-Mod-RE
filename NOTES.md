Unofficial That Sky Mod update for Sky 0.35.2 (Android, Canvas / Sky Modloader).

- Rainbow Trails and Bubble Trails (Player) work again, through substitutes (from Offset Lab): Rainbow Trails gives
  you the game's rainbow trail spell (off removes it); Bubble Trails places the game's bubble zone where you stand
  (it stays until you change level).
- Fix: Disable Joinable (Social) no longer writes into the start of the game library (its game address isn't known
  for this Sky version); it still sends its request to the server.

That Sky Mod is made by XeTrinityz and the TSM team. Unofficial build; see the README for credits.
