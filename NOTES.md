Unofficial That Sky Mod update for Sky 0.35.2 (Android, Canvas / Sky Modloader).

- New: **Skip Spirit Tree Prerequisites** (Unlocks → Temporary Unlocks), from Offset Lab. Lets you select and buy
  spirit tree nodes in any order, including the tiers of seasonal spirits. Turn it on, then close and reopen the
  spirit's tree. It's off every time the game starts. Buying still goes to the server, which may refuse a node.

That Sky Mod is made by XeTrinityz and the TSM team. Unofficial build; see the README for credits.
