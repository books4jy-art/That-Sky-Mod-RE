Unofficial That Sky Mod update for Sky 0.35.5 (Android, Canvas / Sky Modloader).

- **Fix: Auto Wax Tools run** now waits at each plant cluster until the plants have finished burning before moving on.
- **Fix: Auto Eden** deposits your wing light again and reloads your account at the end, so no relogging is needed.
- **Fix: Auto Complete Quests** no longer repeats quests you already finished when you change level.
- **Saved outfits** (Outfits → Save Current Outfit) work again.
- **Known issues on Sky 0.35.5:** Pause UI doesn't work. Unlock All doesn't show emotes you don't own yet. Unlight
  Avatar doesn't work. Kill Avatar and Break Elder Armor are untested.
