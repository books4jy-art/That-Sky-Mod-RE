Unofficial That Sky Mod update for Sky 0.35.2 (Android, Canvas / Sky Modloader).

- **New: Crab burrow** (Player → spawns): choose how many crabs (1–8), their size, distance in front of you, type
  (Normal / Hermit / Zombie) and mood (Neutral / Friendly / Dark), then Spawn Crabs; Remove Crabs makes them burrow
  away. Dark crabs knock you over (turn on Invincibility first if you like).
- **Fix: Disable Cutscenes** (World) now skips cutscenes.
- **Fix: Auto Burn Plants and Auto Burn Dyes** can be on at the same time; turning one off no longer stops the other.

That Sky Mod is made by XeTrinityz and the TSM team. Unofficial build; see the README for credits.
