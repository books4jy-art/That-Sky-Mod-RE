Unofficial That Sky Mod update for Sky 0.35.2 (Android, Canvas / Sky Modloader).

- **Crab Burrow is now a mini menu** (Player → spawns, under Spawn Giant Crab): tap **Crab Burrow** to open or close
  its controls. The open controls sit in a lighter panel with a thin border in your accent colour.
